Project 001 · Synthetic health data consulting

Emergency visits, hospitalization and length of stay

Project 001 generates synthetic NACRS-style emergency records (30,000 visits, 39 variables) and DAD-style inpatient records (60,000 stays, 60 variables) in one shared release (2.0) used by all ten memos, so linked questions can be studied without real patients. The 30 hospital names are real Alberta ED reference labels only; no result describes their actual performance. It is a fictional consulting demonstration — not a real contract or patient population.

  • Question 1: do CTAS level and pre-disposition time affect whether an emergency patient is hospitalized?
  • Question 2: among admitted patients, how do acuity and emergency stay relate to inpatient length of stay?
  • Question 3: for adults whose ED visit is for asthma, how does admission risk compare with similar patients at the same hospital, and does it differ across the Calgary, Edmonton and other regions?
  • Reproducible R workflow with renv, a variable dictionary and example reports

Reference · Interactive site

Alberta Health Data Atlas

An interactive learning atlas of nine commonly used Alberta administrative health datasets and clinical information systems. It is the reference Project 001 was designed from.

  • DAD, NACRS, Practitioner Claims, Lab, PIN, Vital Statistics, Registry, Sunrise Clinical Manager, Connect Care
  • Deep dives on DAD, NACRS and PIN, with field maps and fictional example records
  • Cohort design, linkage notes, access pathways and a responsible-use checklist
