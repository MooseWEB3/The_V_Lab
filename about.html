About The V Lab

An independent lab for learning and practising health data science, with a focus on Alberta.

What we do

The V Lab writes long-form, self-contained learning guides on the methods health data work depends on — biostatistics, epidemiology, visualization and reproducible reporting — and applies them in projects built around Alberta's administrative health data.

Every page and analysis is open on GitHub.

How we work

  • Synthetic, never real. Projects use synthetic or public data only; no patient-level records are published.
  • Reproducible. Analyses are written in R Markdown with pinned environments so they can be rebuilt.
  • Sourced. Dataset descriptions point back to the official custodians rather than copying their documents.
  • Educational. Nothing here is clinical, legal or data-access advice.
