Project 001

Synthetic Alberta emergency & inpatient data

A consulting-style demonstration built on fully synthetic NACRS and DAD records — no real patients — studying two linked questions in order.

  • Does triage acuity (CTAS) and time to disposition predict hospitalization?
  • Among those admitted, what drives inpatient length of stay?
  • Seven consulting memos (A00–A06) and runnable R examples
